DAY BY DAY

Itinerary overview

A clear route designed to connect Ecuador’s most iconic Andean highlights.

Day 1

Arrival in Quito

Airport welcome and transfer to your hotel in Quito. Free time depending on arrival.

Day 2

Colonial Quito & Middle of the World

Historic center, San Francisco Church, La Compañía de Jesús, Equator Monument and Intiñan Museum.

Day 3

Otavalo Market & Peguche Waterfall

Visit the traditional artisan market and walk to the sacred Peguche Waterfall.

Day 4

Quilotoa Lagoon & Andean Culture

Toachi Canyon, Tigua Workshop, panoramic crater views and time around the volcanic lagoon.

Day 5

Cotopaxi National Park

Limpiopungo Lagoon and your choice of volcano refuge trekking or horseback riding before continuing to Baños.

Day 6

Baños Waterfall Route

Manto de la Novia, Pailón del Diablo and Casa del Árbol.

Day 7

Free morning in Baños & return to Quito

Free time in Baños before your road transfer back to Quito, the airport area or your next destination.
